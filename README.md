# scatlasexploration
This is an exploration of the Social Capital Atlas from Harvard University's Opportunity Insights Lab. This exploration is intended to benchmark the Houston metropolitan area against peer areas.


# Purpose:
The objective of this overview is to analyze the impact of social capital on the outcomes of populations within the 5 largest metropolitan areas. In addition, this experiment will compare the results to the smallest 5 metropolitan areas. The results are compared to each other for the purpose of reviewing the effect of population density on the social mobility of populations.

# Source: 
The Social Capital Atlas Research Report from Harvard's Opportunity Insights Lab. 

The data is sourced from the following sources:
1.) 72.2 million Facebook user Friendship Data
2.) U.S. Census Bureau - American Community Survey 
3.) Tax data for the Upward Mobility based on area data (Zip Code)
4.) Opportunity Atlas Datasets on Children's Outcomes



# Scope: 
- Economic Connectedness measurements for Black, White, and Hispanic students and their parents comparison across the top 5 and lowest 5 metropolitan statistical areas.


# Procedures:
- Reproduce the scatterplots of the distributions first to understand why these matter to document understanding of the charts and to interpret the measures.
- Identifying the metropolitan areas for comparison: Top 5 and Bottom 5 metropolitan statistical areas according to the U.S. Census Survey
